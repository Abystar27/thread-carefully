Project Re:claim
The SATCoL Visit — Polyester recycling & circular economy

As part of the research behind Thread Carefully, the team visited Project Re:claim — the UK's first commercial-scale post-consumer polyester recycling facility, run by the Salvation Army Trading Company Limited (SATCoL) and Plan B Circular Workwear.

♻️
About the Facility
What Project Re:claim Does

Project Re:claim takes post-consumer polyester garments — donated through SATCoL's vast charity collection network — and transforms them into polyester pellets that can re-enter the manufacturing cycle as raw material.

It represents a critical shift: rather than exporting waste or sending garments to landfill, the facility creates a domestic circular loop. Yet the market for recycled polyester remains largely unproven, and public awareness is still low — making commercial viability one of the biggest ongoing challenges.

Student Reflections
What the Team Learned

Several members of the Thread Carefully team — MSc Global Business Management students at the University of Roehampton — visited the facility and shared their reflections.

"Seeing this process showed me how technology and innovation can help make production and consumption more sustainable — but recycling is not always a business option. We need business plans, stakeholder engagement, and market incentives to support long-term sustainability goals."
Toyyibat Abisola Alao · MSc Global Business Management
"A key lesson was that innovation alone does not guarantee sustainability success. Achieving a circular economy requires more than technological innovation — it requires consumer education, stakeholder collaboration, and market development."
Oluwafemi Oshofisan · MSc Global Business Management
"This experience bridges academic theory and real-world application. It transforms the concept of a circular economy into an operational reality — proving that sustainable supply chains require proactive, upstream product design rather than end-of-life damage control."
Daniel Odunlami · MSc Global Business Management
"The visit challenged my understanding of sustainability by showing how circular economy principles can be applied in practice. I was particularly interested in how the Circular Textiles Foundation works with manufacturers and retailers to influence product design at the production stage."
Emmanuel Chisom Ogbogu-Fidelis · MSc Global Business Management
